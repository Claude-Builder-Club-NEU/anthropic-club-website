## Claude news this month

### Two new models

Fable 5.1 and Mythos 5.1, announced September 1. Same underlying model,
different safeguards: Fable 5.1 is generally available, while Mythos 5.1 is
limited to trusted access programs, with safeguards built for cybersecurity and
life sciences work.

Fable 5.1 is designed for complex, long-running tasks, and its research
capabilities offer an early look at how models will contribute to scientific
progress. It sets a new standard across Anthropic's benchmarks, and at lower
effort levels it matches or beats Fable 5 at much lower cost.

### Watermarking

Anything from a Claude product released after August 2 carries machine-readable
marking, to satisfy EU AI Act Article 50(2). Older models have until December 2.

### Claude has two new browsers

Claude in Chrome is now available on every paid plan, and it now acts
autonomously instead of asking permission for each step, with a classifier
checking each action against what you asked. The same day, Cowork got a browser
inside the desktop app that opens in the side panel and works a site for you. It
is Claude's browser, not yours, so it never sees your tabs or passwords.

### Memory is shared and editable

Chat and Cowork now use one memory, and Claude adds to it while you chat rather
than after. Every memory is a short file under Topics in settings that you can
read, edit, or delete. Health, religion, politics, and similar topics stay out
unless you opt in.

### Also this month

- Claude Academy launched, and Claude for Teachers became free for US K-12
  districts.
- Claude Tag reads the whole channel instead of one message at a time, about 30%
  better at knowing when to stay quiet.
- Claude Security scans now run on Mythos 5 for Enterprise, and a new $35M fund
  backs open-source security work.
- Inference hooks (inline DLP), self-hosted Claude Code environments, and
  Compliance API coverage for Cowork and Claude Code all entered beta.
- Claude Code: the `/design` research preview brings editable artboards into the
  CLI.
- Claude Code: Remote Control left preview, and your machine now shows up as a
  device card in the mobile Code tab.
- Claude Code: sessions continue automatically when a usage limit resets.

## We have a new website

We recently rebuilt our website to focus on community building.

**Everything we run lives on [/events](/events).** Date, time, room, and an RSVP
button for that specific session.

**Check in at [/attendance](/attendance).** There is a six-character code on the
screen at every session. Enter it with your name and your Northeastern email,
and you collect a stamp: eight to a card, a fresh card each term. There is no
account and no password, so your email is your card. Use the same address every
week, or you have quietly started a second one. The code expires shortly after
we start, so check in while you are still in the room.

**To understand what our community truly wants, we launched [/polls](/polls).**
During a session, we open a ballot: rank the workshops you want, tell us what
times actually work, say which socials you would genuinely turn up to.

**Pitch a workshop at [/events/pitch](/events/pitch).** If you can build
something in an hour, you can teach it. No expertise required, no slides yet,
and no need to work out which board member to email. Five questions, about two
minutes, straight to our inbox.
