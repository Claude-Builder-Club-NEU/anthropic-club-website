**Who we are.** The Claude Builders Club is a student-led chapter of
Anthropic's Claude Builder Club program, founded at Northeastern in Fall 2025,
with members from every college on campus.

**What members get.** Claude API credits, Claude Pro, merch, workshop
materials, and a direct line to the campus team.

**How workshops run.** We teach it, you build it, you show it off. Workshops run
every other week and are labeled by level, so you can come to the ones that fit
where you are.

out on top, followed by Financial modeling with Claude and Building a business
with Claude Code.

## Claude news this month

### Two new models

Claude Opus 5.5, announced September 22, is the first model in the new 5.5
family. It performs at the level of Fable 5.1 on most work, costs 40% less to
run than Opus 5, and writes its answers about 30% faster.

Claude Sonnet 5.5 followed on September 28. It is the fastest Sonnet yet, keeps
the same price as Sonnet 5, and is available on every plan, including Free.

### Chat and Cowork are now one Claude

On September 16, chat and Cowork merged. Pro and Max users no longer pick a
mode: Claude decides whether a task needs a quick answer or a longer job that
keeps going after you close your laptop. It is rolling out over the next few
weeks.

### Docs, Slides and Design in any conversation

The same day, Claude Docs and Claude Slides launched, and Claude Design moved
into conversations. You can build documents, decks, and designs right in the
chat, edit them, and leave comments for Claude to act on. Available on every
plan, including Free.

### Claude found a new enzyme system

About 950 Claude agents searched a large DNA sequence database for 21 hours and
turned up a previously unknown enzyme system in viruses that infect bacteria,
with DNA repeats that resemble CRISPR. Anthropic's lab then confirmed it in
experiments.

### Also this month

- Projects were redesigned to work like a conversation instead of a folder.
- A new Claude Marketplace lists plugins, agents, and partner services, and a
  developer portal lets anyone submit a plugin to the Claude directory.
- Anthropic published its latest report on detecting and countering misuse of
  Claude.
- Claude for Government became generally available.
- Claude Code: Opus 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5 are the new defaults, and
  `claude --desktop` opens the desktop app on your current folder.
