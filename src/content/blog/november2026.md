## HACK1984 recap

On November 7 and 8, Claude Builders Club, ACM, REV and AINU took over 300 Mass
Ave for HACK1984, Northeastern's largest hackathon ever. Over 24 hours, 83
participants built 32 projects and competed for over $14k in prizes. Thank you
to everyone who came out, built something and stayed up way too late with us.

### The winners

Two projects stole the show: **BlindSpot** took 1st in two different tracks,
and **DoubleThink** walked away with two prizes.

**Privacy-First** (main track)

1. **BlindSpot**, the medical AI that can't see your patients. (2 wins)
2. **DuckyCode**
3. **HuskyHealth**

## Claude news this month

### Anthropic filed to go public

On October 26, Anthropic made its IPO paperwork (an S-1) public. It plans to
list on Nasdaq under the ticker **ANT**, and the filing shows **$16.3 billion in
revenue in the first half of 2026**, up from $1.2 billion a year earlier. Our
favorite detail: Claude wrote more than 90% of the contributions to Anthropic's
own codebase in September. On November 10, Anthropic started its IPO roadshow,
the round of investor meetings that comes right before a company's shares start
trading. ([The Batch](https://www.deeplearning.ai/the-batch/anthropic-opens-its-books/),
[Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news))

### Claude Duet: Claude messages you first

Regular Claude waits for you to start a chat. **Claude Duet**, a new app
announced on October 21, flips that. It keeps an eye on the apps you connect,
like email and calendar, prepares work for you, and pings you when something
needs your attention. It asks for your OK before anything sensitive, like
sending a message or making a payment. It runs on Claude Opus 5.5 and is a
research preview with a waitlist for Pro and Max subscribers in the US.
([Claude](https://claude.com/resources/articles/claude-duet))

### Two new models

**Claude Haiku 5.5** (October 7) is Anthropic's cheapest, fastest small model
yet, and costs about 75% less to run than Haiku 4.5. **Claude Fable 5.2**
(November 9) is the new top model for the hardest work, with better coding,
reasoning and vision, and output up to 30% faster than Fable 5.1. Its sibling,
Claude Mythos 5.2, is limited to vetted organizations.
([Haiku 5.5](https://anthropic.com/claude-haiku-5-5),
[Fable 5.2](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-fable-and-mythos-5-2))

### Slides, Docs and Design are free for everyone

On October 8, Claude Slides, Docs and Design came out of beta and landed on
every plan, including Free. That's good news for Thursday: you don't need a paid
plan to build your deck. Anthropic also launched two new betas, Dashboards and
Motion. ([Claude](https://claude.com/resources/articles/dashboards-and-motion))
