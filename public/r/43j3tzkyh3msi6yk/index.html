Why the four early ballots are tests. Each one uses a workshop option that isn’t in the live poll (claude-code, side-projects or prompting), and none of the 91 session ballots does. One also shares its timestamp with a batch of load-test rows. Leaving them out changes no ranking on this page. It only affects the hackathon, where it breaks a tie.

58 ballots came in between 6 and 7 PM ET and 33 between 7 and 8 PM. Timestamps are stored rounded to the hour.

Public page

claudeneu.com/polls currently reads “Money Moves won with 26% of 95 ballots.” Those 95 include the four test ballots, and with them Money Moves and Fix Northeastern are tied 25–25. The page names Money Moves only because of how it breaks ties. Once the test ballots are removed it will read 27% of 91, which is correct.

Next steps

What we recommend

  • Clean the ballots table before announcing anything.Delete the four test ballots so the public page shows 91 ballots, then clear the 1,651 load and smoke-test rows.
  • Book Financial Modeling, Business, then Student Agents.Hold First App as the alternate if a date or presenter falls through.
  • Run Money Moves as the hackathon theme, loosely.The result is too close to treat as a mandate, so the theme can flex if a partner club or sponsor needs something else.
  • Open four build-team tracks and plan for ML overflow.Machine learning, Investment, Engineering and Rotating. Reconsider Biology or fold it into the rotating track.
  • Lead the calendar with partner-club socials.Run one coworking session as a pilot before committing to a weekly slot.
  • Test future polls under their own poll name.That keeps pre-launch votes out of the public results.

Read these results with care

  • Voters are the people who came to the info session, not the whole membership.
  • With 91 ballots, any single-answer share could be off by about 10 points either way.
  • “I would apply” at an info session is a statement of intent. Expect fewer real applications.
  • Ballots are anonymous, and only the browser remembering a vote prevents voting twice. No two ballots are identical, but that doesn’t rule it out.
